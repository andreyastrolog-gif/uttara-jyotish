01 Work, career, life purpose

Career and life purpose: finding your calling

“Career Search: Your Path to Success” consultation

€120

Book now

Sound familiar?

  • “I do everything right, but my work brings neither joy nor the income I need.”
  • “The years go by, and I still don’t know what my calling is.”
  • “Every morning I force myself to go somewhere I feel like an outsider.”
  • “I want to change careers or start my own business, but I’m afraid of getting it wrong.”

If nothing changes

Years go into work that isn’t yours. Fatigue builds up and turns into burnout, your income hits the same ceiling again and again, and decisions about changing careers are made at random — under the pressure of circumstances or other people’s advice. Over time, the feeling grows that life is passing you by, and changing anything gets harder and harder.

What your chart will show and how the consultation works

Your birth chart shows what kind of work suits your nature. I look at the lagna (ascendant) and the lords of the 1st and 10th houses, the Atmakaraka and Amatyakaraka, the divisional chart of career — the Dashamsha (D10) — as well as signs and nakshatras. This reveals your strengths and weaknesses in work and business, the kind of activity that fits you, and the areas where you can fulfil yourself most completely.

We also look at the periods of your life: when it’s best to change direction, study or start your own business. You ask your own questions — about your current job, business or studies — and we connect them to your chart.

What you’ll get

  • An understanding of your svadharma — the work that is truly yours: “one profession, but truly your own”
  • A clear picture of your strengths and the fields that suit you
  • An understanding of which steps make sense now and which are better postponed
  • Calm confidence in your decisions instead of going back and forth
  • A video recording of the consultation and a written chart analysis

02 Relocation

Relocation: where you’ll live and work best

“Relocation: Your Ideal Place” consultation

Book now

Sound familiar?

  • “We moved, but things didn’t get easier — it’s as if nothing works out.”
  • “I’m choosing between several cities and just can’t make up my mind.”
  • “I feel boxed in where I live: neither work nor relationships are working out.”
  • “Moving is too big a step to take at random.”

If nothing changes

Moving is one of the most costly decisions in life: money, energy, connections, years of adjusting. If you choose a place at random, old difficulties may repeat themselves in the new one, and the next move will take even more resources. And sometimes people stay for years where life is hard for them, simply because they can’t see where to go.

What your chart will show and how the consultation works

Your birth chart plays out differently in different parts of the world. Using astrocartography (ACG lines), I look at where the planets connected with work, income, relationships and inner peace are stronger, and where their influence is more challenging.

During the consultation, we compare the specific cities and countries you are considering, choose a direction, and look at how to bring out the strengths of the place where you live now — so you can make the most of them.

What you’ll get

  • An understanding of where life can become more harmonious, work more successful, and rest truly enjoyable
  • A comparison of your relocation options based on your chart
  • The strengths of your current place and ways to use them
  • A confident, well-considered decision instead of a lottery
  • A video recording of the consultation and a written chart analysis

03 Family, synastry, compatibility, marriage

Compatibility and marriage: understanding each other

“Marital Compatibility: An Astrological Analysis” consultation

€110

Book now

Sound familiar?

  • “We love each other, but we keep arguing about the same things.”
  • “I don’t understand what my partner really expects from me.”
  • “The same scenario keeps repeating in my relationships.”
  • “Are we ready for marriage — or should we wait?”

If nothing changes

Differences that aren’t understood turn into chronic conflicts over time. Resentment and distance build up, and important decisions — about marriage, children or separation — are made on emotion. And an unresolved pattern often carries over into the next relationship and affects the children.

What your chart will show and how the consultation works

Synastry is the comparison of two people’s birth charts. I look at Moon compatibility, how the planets in the partners’ charts interact, the relationship axes and the Navamsha (D9) — the chart of marriage and partnership. We look at which aspects of your interaction are strong and which are tense, what lies behind the dissatisfaction and how to balance it.

We speak openly: my job is not to sugar-coat or to frighten, but to show how to build a strong relationship that respects each partner’s nature. A business partnership can be analysed in a similar way.

What you’ll get

  • An understanding of your own needs and tasks in the relationship — and of your partner’s nature
  • Clarity on what to emphasise and what to soften in your interaction
  • An understanding of how to avoid unwanted scenarios in family life
  • A solid basis for calm, conscious decisions about your future as a couple
  • A video recording of the consultation and a written analysis of both charts

  • “There are problems at school, and I don’t understand why.”
  • “I’m afraid of imposing my expectations and breaking what’s special in them.”
  • “Which activities and which direction should we choose so as not to get it wrong?”

If nothing changes

Parenting by guesswork — through pressure, comparison with others and your own fears — hurts both the child and the parents. Talents stay undiscovered, and the time when they are easiest to develop slips away. Misunderstanding grows and later carries over into adult life.

What your chart will show and how the consultation works

A child’s chart shows their character, talents and vulnerable sides. I look at which houses and planets deserve attention, the planetary periods in the child’s life, the D12 chart — how the family reflects the child’s karma — and the stages of education: abilities and the choice of activities and direction.

Important: not every challenging indicator in a chart means problems. We look for real potential for growth and for ways to support your child in line with their nature, rather than through fear or parental expectations.

What you’ll get

  • An understanding of the wishes and tasks your child came into this world with
  • A clear picture of their strengths and vulnerabilities
  • Ideas for education, activities and choosing a direction
  • Ways to help without pressure or fear — and warmer relationships in the family
  • A video recording of the consultation and a written chart analysis

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions about Vedic astrology consultations

Can a birth chart show my life purpose and the right career?

Yes, this is one of the main areas of my work. In Vedic astrology, life purpose (svadharma) and career are read from the lagna (ascendant), the lords of the 1st and 10th houses, the Atmakaraka and Amatyakaraka, and the Dashamsha (D10) — the divisional chart of career. The chart shows your strengths, the kind of work that suits you and the periods that favour change. The decision is always yours — astrology helps you make it consciously. The “Career Search” consultation costs €120.

How does Vedic astrology help choose a city or country to move to?

Your birth chart plays out differently in different parts of the world. Astrocartography (ACG lines) shows where the themes of work, income, relationships and inner peace are stronger. In the “Relocation” consultation (€80), we compare the specific options you are considering and look at the strengths of the place where you live now.

How do I check a couple’s compatibility, and should we get married?

This is done with synastry — a comparison of both partners’ birth charts. I look at Moon compatibility, how the planets interact, the relationship axes and the Navamsha (D9). You’ll learn which aspects of your interaction are strong and which are tense, and how to avoid unwanted scenarios in family life. The “Marital Compatibility” consultation costs €110.

Why do parents need a children’s horoscope?

A children’s horoscope helps you understand your child’s character, talents and vulnerable sides, choose suitable activities and a direction for their education, and support them in line with their nature — without pressure or fear. Importantly, not every challenging indicator in a chart means problems: we look for real potential for growth. The price is on request — message me on Telegram.

How much does a Vedic astrology consultation cost?

Prices in euros: “Career Search” — €120, “Relocation” — €80, “Marital Compatibility” — €110, “12 Areas of Destiny” — €100, “Periods of Destiny” — €140. The price of a children’s horoscope consultation is on request.

How does an online consultation work, and what will I get afterwards?

You click “Book now” and a Telegram bot opens. You write your name and choose a topic, and I reply to you personally right there on Telegram: we discuss the topic of the consultation and agree on a convenient time. The consultation takes place online and is recorded on video — you can rewatch it as often as you like. Afterwards you also receive written materials with a detailed analysis of your horoscope: planets, zodiac signs, houses. You don’t need to know astrology beforehand: I explain things in a structured way, without complicated esoteric jargon.

What information do you need to read my chart?

To build a chart, I need your date, time and place of birth. For compatibility — the data of both partners; for a children’s horoscope — the child’s data; for relocation — also the cities or countries you are considering. We’ll clarify all the details when you book.

What language are consultations in, and can I book from another country?

I work in Russian and Ukrainian. Consultations take place online, so you can book from any country.
